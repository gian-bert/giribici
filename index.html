Credits & licenses

Biblioteca giri bici — an offline, private library for road-bike tours. Made by gianbert. This app is built on open software and open data; the sources below are credited as their licenses require.

  • Leaflet — map engine (bundled), © Volodymyr Agafonkin & Leaflet contributors, BSD-2-Clause.
  • swisstopo — "swisstopo" map tiles & pass names/positions © Federal Office of Topography swisstopo, used under its open-government-data terms.
  • Natural Earth — country borders & base geography, public domain.
  • GeoNames — town locations, CC-BY 4.0.
  • Mountain passes — verified against OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and national open data (e.g. swisstopo OGD); the remainder are named cycling climbs whose locations are public geographic facts, with elevations cross-checked against public references.
  • Weather — forecasts by WeatherAPI.com, used under its free plan (commercial use permitted). Attribution "Powered by WeatherAPI.com" is shown with each forecast.
  • Lake outlines — hand-traced from documented dimensions for this app.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Your tours stay in this browser; online features (weather, shared-library, live position, map tiles) are optional.

When you use an online feature, only the data it needs leaves your device: route points & times go to WeatherAPI.com (forecasts), map-tile requests go to swisstopo, and the shared-library file is fetched from where you host it. Your location, if enabled, is used on your device. No personal data is collected, kept on our side, or sold. Full privacy policy.
